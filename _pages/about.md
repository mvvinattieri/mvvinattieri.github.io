Welcome to my personal webpage! My name is Maria Veronica Vinattieri, and I am a Biostatistician with a Ph.D. in Statistics. My commitment lies in putting Statistics at service of Healthcare, with the aim of improving people's health.

I have done a Postdoc at [Karolinska Institutet](https://ki.se/en) with focus on statistical methods in breast cancer epidemiology and screening at the Department of Medical Epidemiology and Biostatistics. Visit my KI webpage [here](https://ki.se/en/people/maria-veronica-vinattieri).
